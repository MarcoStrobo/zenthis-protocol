FAQ

Questions

Do I need to pay anything to participate? ▾
No. Testnet ETH is free from any Sepolia faucet. You only need gas to interact with the testnet, and Sepolia gas is free.
How is my testnet activity verified? ▾
The ZenthisHTLC contract on Sepolia emits events for every swap. We index these on-chain events before the snapshot. Your testnet address must have at least one confirmed transaction with the Zenthis contract.
Can I use any wallet? ▾
Yes. Any EVM-compatible wallet works on Sepolia testnet: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, hardware wallets via MetaMask, etc. For receiving the airdrop on mainnet, we recommend a hardware wallet for security.
Is the airdrop contract audited? ▾
The ZENTHIS token and HTLC contracts are published on GitHub. An external audit is scheduled before mainnet deployment. The audit report will be published on zenthisprotocol.xyz before TGE.
What if I registered but had no testnet transactions? ▾
Registrations without any on-chain testnet activity are excluded from the airdrop automatically. You must complete at least one swap on the Sepolia testnet app before June 10, 2026.
